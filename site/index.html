Sol

One window for all your AI, on your own Mac. Your chats, your coding agents, your browser, a desk, your voice, and the tools you make, in one place that is yours.

Mac, the line

curl -fsSL https://sol.js.org/install | sh

Paste it in Terminal. It asks what to call you, puts Sol in ~/Sol and opens it. A few minutes, once. No GitHub account, no Homebrew.

Mac, the download

download Sol for Mac

Unzip, open Sol. It asks the same question and sets itself up. Sol is not yet signed with Apple, so the first time macOS stops and asks once:

Linux, and Windows under WSL

curl -fsSL https://sol.js.org/install | sh

The same line; no app there, Sol opens in your browser. Windows: inside Ubuntu under WSL (wsl --install once in PowerShell).

Sol is in beta. The code: github.com/marcocyl04-ux/sol-beta. Something wrong, or a thought: open an issue there.
